# erdos41 — GPU Quantitative Analysis of Erdős Problem #41 (Lower-Envelope Density of Infinite $B _ 3$ Sequences)

A hybrid Rust + CUDA toolkit analyzing **Erdős Problem #41** (500 USD open prize; [erdosproblems.com/41](https://www.erdosproblems.com/41)): prove or disprove that every infinite $B _ 3$ sequence $A \subseteq \mathbb{N}$ (all 3-term sums $a _ i + a _ j + a _ k$ with $i \le j \le k$ distinct) satisfies

```
lim inf_{x -> infinity} A(x) / x^(1/3) = 0,
```

where $A(x) = \lvert A \cap [1, x]\rvert$ is the prefix counting function of $A$ (OEIS [A004023](https://oeis.org/A004023) greedy $B _ 3$ sequence; optimal finite $B _ 3$ rulers [A051912](https://oeis.org/A051912)).

## Novel Mathematical & Computational Contributions

1. **Proposition 2.1 (Classical $\text{Even-}h$ Difference-Harmonic Identity vs. $\text{Odd-}h$ Asymmetric Difference Barrier; Erdős 1955, Nash 1989/1995, Jia 1993):**
   Explicitly contrasts why $2k$-sum collisions in $B _ {2k}$ rearrange into symmetric $k$-fold differences $(k A) - (k A){}$ with cancelling leading scales, whereas $B _ 3$ 3-sum collisions rearrange only into asymmetric $(2,1){}$-differences $w _ {i,j,k} = a _ k - a _ i - a _ j \in A - (A + A){}$ of scale $\Theta(a _ k){}$.
2. **Theorem 2.1 (Exact Non-Asymptotic $(2,1){}$-Difference Harmonic Conservation Identity & Dyadic Shell Inequality):**
   - **Theorem 2.1(a) (Injectivity & Exact Harmonic Conservation):** For any $B _ 3$ set $A = \lbrace a _ 1 \lt a _ 2 \lt \dots\rbrace \subseteq \mathbb{N}$ and any horizon $N \ge 2$ with $m = A(N){}$, the map $(i, j, k) \mapsto w _ {i,j,k} := a _ k - a _ i - a _ j$ is strictly injective on $\mathcal{T} _ m = \lbrace (i, j, k) : 1 \le i \le j \lt k \le m\rbrace$ of size $\binom{m+1}{3}$, satisfies $\lvert w _ {i,j,k}\rvert + 2 \le a _ k$ everywhere, and obeys the exact conservation identity:
     ```
     \int_1^N \frac{A(t)(A(t)^2 - 1)}{6 t^2} dt + \frac{m(m^2 - 1)}{6 N}
         + \sum_{(i,j,k) \in T_m} \frac{\min(a_i + a_j - 2, 2 a_k - a_i - a_j - 2)}{(|a_k - a_i - a_j| + 2) a_k}
         = \sum_{w \in W_A(N)} \frac{1}{|w| + 2} <= 2 H_N - \frac{5}{2}.
     ```
   - **Theorem 2.1(b)–(c) (Exact Dyadic Shell Inequality & Multi-Scale Resonance Decomposition):** Partitioning $[1, 2 ^ R]$ into dyadic shells $I _ 1 = [1, 2]$ and $I _ r = (2 ^ {r-1}, 2 ^ r]$ ($M _ r = A(2 ^ r){}$), every $(i, j, k) \in \mathcal{T} _ {M _ R}$ with $a _ k \in I _ r$ has minimum shell index $r _ {\min}(w) = \lfloor \log _ 2(\lvert w\rvert+1) \rfloor + 1 \le r$ (with $r _ {\min}(0) = 1$), yielding the exact multi-scale identity:
     ```
     \frac{\binom{M_R + 1}{3}}{2^R} + \sum_{r=1}^{R-1} \frac{\binom{M_r + 1}{3}}{2^{r+1}}
         + \sum_{r=2}^R \sum_{s=1}^{r-1} \frac{C_{r,s}(A)}{2^{r-s+1}}
         = \sum_{w \in W_A(2^R)} 2^{-r_min(w)} <= R - \frac{1}{2},
     ```
     with exact equality $1/2 = 1/2$ at $R = 1$ for $A = \lbrace 1, 2\rbrace$.
3. **Theorem 2.2 (Bose–Chowla $\mathrm{GF}(p ^ 3){}$ Pigeonhole Gap Compression & Cubic Burst–Desert Dichotomy):**
   Constructs modular Bose–Chowla $B _ 3$ sets $S _ p = \lbrace \log _ \theta(\theta + x) : x \in \mathrm{GF}(p)\rbrace \subseteq \mathbb{Z}/(p ^ 3 - 1)\mathbb{Z}$ and proves that cutting $\mathbb{Z}/(p ^ 3 - 1)\mathbb{Z}$ across its largest adjacent modular gap $g _ {\max}(S _ p) \ge \lceil (p ^ 3 - 1)/p \rceil = p ^ 2$ produces an integer $B _ 3$ set $S _ p ^ \ast \subseteq \lbrace 1, \dots, N _ p ^ \ast \rbrace$ of cardinality $p$ with compressed span $N _ p ^ \ast = p ^ 3 - g _ {\max}(S _ p) \le p ^ 3 - p ^ 2$ (e.g. $N _ 5 ^ \ast = 56 \le 100$, $N _ 7 ^ \ast = 240 \le 294$, $N _ {11} ^ \ast = 859 \le 1210$, giving $\Psi(N _ {11} ^ \ast ) = 11 / 859 ^ {1/3} = 1.1572 \gt 1$).
4. **Theorem 2.3 (Sign-Split Positive/Negative $(2,1){}$-Cone Conservation & Intra-Shell Negative-Cone Exclusivity):**
   Partitions $\mathcal{T} _ m = \mathcal{T} _ m ^ + \sqcup \mathcal{T} _ m ^ 0 \sqcup \mathcal{T} _ m ^ -$ by the sign of $w _ {i,j,k} = a _ k - a _ i - a _ j$, proving that $\lvert \mathcal{T} _ m ^ 0\rvert \le 1$, that the Positive Cone $\mathcal{T} _ m ^ +$ ($a _ i + a _ j \lt a _ k$) and Negative Cone $\mathcal{T} _ m ^ -$ ($a _ i + a _ j \gt a _ k$) obey separate exact conservation identities $\mathcal{E} _ A ^ \pm(N) + \Delta _ A ^ \pm(N) = \mathcal{B} _ A ^ \pm(N) \le H _ N - 3/2$ (halving the two-cone ceiling $2 H _ N - 5/2$), and that **100% of intra-shell triples** $2 ^ {r-1} \lt a _ i \le a _ j \lt a _ k \le 2 ^ r$ ($r \ge 2$) satisfy $w _ {i,j,k} \le -2$ and land inside the Negative Cone $\mathcal{W} _ A ^ -(2 ^ R){}$ with dyadic ceiling $(R - 1)/2$.
5. **Theorem 2.4 (Cubic-Beta Radial Reduction, Closed-Form Fermat-Cone Integral, and Unconditional Vanishing of Regular Asymptotic Cubic Density):**
   Proves via the Beta-function identity $B(1/3, 1/3) = \Gamma(1/3) ^ 2 / \Gamma(2/3){}$ that on the Positive Quantile Cone $\Delta ^ + = \lbrace (x,y) : 0 \lt x \le y,\; x ^ 3 + y ^ 3 \lt 1\rbrace$ of area $\lvert \Delta ^ +\rvert = \frac{\Gamma(1/3) ^ 2}{12\,\Gamma(2/3)} \approx 0.4416596876$ ($88.3319\%$ of the ordered index simplex), every radial integral reduces to $\iint _ {\Delta ^ +} f(x ^ 3+y ^ 3)\,dx\,dy = \frac{\Gamma(1/3) ^ 2}{18\,\Gamma(2/3)}\int _ 0 ^ 1 r ^ {-1/3} f(r)\,dr$, yielding the exact closed-form Fermat-Cone Integral ($z _ \varepsilon = (1-\varepsilon) ^ {1/3}$):
   ```
   I_+(eps) := \iint_{x^3 + y^3 <= 1 - eps} \frac{dx dy}{1 - x^3 - y^3}
             = \frac{\Gamma(1/3)^2}{18 \Gamma(2/3)} \left[ \frac{1}{2} \ln \frac{1 + z_eps + z_eps^2}{(1 - z_eps)^2}
               - \sqrt{3} \arctan \frac{2 z_eps + 1}{\sqrt{3}} + \frac{\pi}{2\sqrt{3}} \right]
   ```
   and proving unconditionally that if $\lim _ {x\to\infty} A(x)/x ^ {1/3} = c$ exists for a $B _ 3$ sequence $A$, then $c \le c _ {\max}(\varepsilon) := (3 / \mathcal{I} _ +(\varepsilon)) ^ {1/3} \to 0$, so $c = 0$.
6. **Theorem 2.5 (Full-Cube $(2,1){}$-Difference Injectivity, Exact Symmetric Variance Identity $\mathrm{Var} _ {\mathcal{I} _ m}(w) = \frac{3(m+1)}{m-1}\sigma _ A ^ 2$, and Neyman–Pearson Quantile-Dominance $\liminf$ Bound):**
   - **Full-Cube Injectivity ($3\times$ the Ordered Cone):** Proves that for any $B _ 3$ set $A = \lbrace a _ 1 \lt \dots \lt a _ m\rbrace$ ($m \ge 3$), the map $w(i, j; k) := a _ i + a _ j - a _ k$ is **100% injective** on the full index set $\mathcal{I} _ m := \lbrace (i, j, k) \in \lbrace 1, \dots, m\rbrace ^ 3 : 1 \le i \le j \le m,\; k \notin \lbrace i, j\rbrace \rbrace$ of cardinality $T _ m = \frac{m ^ 2(m-1)}{2}$ ($3\times$ the ordered cone $\binom{m+1}{3} \sim \frac{m ^ 3}{6}$).
   - **Exact Symmetric First- & Second-Moment Identities:** Proves that for any real sequence $a _ 1, \dots, a _ m$ ($m \ge 3$) with mean $\bar{a}$ and population variance $\sigma _ A ^ 2 = \frac{1}{m}\sum _ {r=1} ^ m (a _ r - \bar{a}) ^ 2$,
     ```
     \frac{1}{T_m} \sum_{(i,j,k) \in I_m} w(i, j; k) = \bar{a},
     \sum_{(i,j,k) \in I_m} (w(i, j; k) - \bar{a})^2 = \frac{3 m (m + 1)}{2} \sum_{r=1}^m (a_r - \bar{a})^2,
     \mathrm{Var}_{I_m}(w(i, j; k)) = \frac{3(m + 1)}{m - 1} \sigma_A^2,
     ```
     yielding the universal $\text{finite-}B _ 3$ variance lower bound $\sigma _ A ^ 2 \ge \frac{(m-1)(T _ m ^ 2 - 1)}{36(m+1)} \sim \frac{m ^ 6}{144}$.
   - **Neyman–Pearson Quantile-Dominance Envelope:** Proves that every measurable function $0 \le f(x) \le x ^ 3$ on $[0, 1]$ has variance at most $\kappa _ \ast := \max _ {t \in [0,1]} \left[\frac{1 - t ^ 7}{7} - \left(\frac{1 - t ^ 4}{4}\right) ^ 2\right] \approx 0.0951109395$, attained at the unique root $t _ \ast \approx 0.7166727493$ of the irreducible quartic $t _ \ast ^ 4 + 2 t _ \ast ^ 3 - 1 = 0$, implying the unconditional $\liminf$ bound $\liminf _ {x\to\infty} A(x)/x ^ {1/3} \le (144\,\kappa _ \ast ) ^ {1/6} \approx 1.54679287 \lt 4 ^ {1/3} \approx 1.58740105$.
7. **Theorem 2.6 (One-Sided Positive-Definite Sub-Cone $\mathcal{I} _ m ^ { \lt }$ Conservation & Completely Monotone Laplace–Abel $\liminf$ Bound):**
   Proves that on the sub-cone $\mathcal{I} _ m ^ { \lt } := \lbrace (i, j, k) \in \mathcal{I} _ m : 1 \le k \lt j\rbrace$ of cardinality $\sum _ {j=2} ^ m (j-1) ^ 2 = \frac{m(m-1)(2m-1)}{6} \sim \frac{m ^ 3}{3}$, **100% of triples satisfy $2 \le w(i, j; k) \le 2 a _ j - 1$** and obey the exact single-harmonic conservation identity
   ```
   \sum_{j=2}^m \frac{(j - 1)^2}{2 a_j} + \sum_{(i,j,k) \in I_m^<} \frac{a_j - a_i + a_k + 1}{2 a_j (a_i + a_j - a_k - 1)}
       = \sum_{(i,j,k) \in I_m^<} \frac{1}{a_i + a_j - a_k - 1} <= H_{2 a_m - 2},
   ```
   and that both Fermat-truncated cone counting on $\mathcal{J} _ M ^ { \lt } = \lbrace (i, j, k) \in \mathcal{I} _ \infty ^ { \lt } : i ^ 3 + j ^ 3 \le M ^ 3\rbrace$ (of normalized volume $\iint _ {0 \lt x \le y,\, x ^ 3+y ^ 3 \le 1} y\,dx\,dy = \frac{\ln 2 + \pi/\sqrt{3}}{9} \approx 0.27854962$) and completely monotone Laplace–Abel summation $\sum _ {\mathcal{I} _ \infty ^ { \lt }} e ^ {-\lambda(a _ i + a _ j - a _ k)} \le \frac{e ^ {-2\lambda}}{1 - e ^ {-\lambda}}$ yield the unconditional closed-form bound

8. **Theorem 2.7 (Exact First-Moment Polynomial Identity on $\mathcal{I} _ m ^ { \lt }$, Concave-Margin Positivity of the Sub-Level Sub-Cone $\Omega _ L ^ \ast$, and Exact Variable-Separated Beta–Gamma Asymptotic $\liminf _ {x\to\infty} A(x)/x ^ {1/3} \le \mathcal{K} _ \ast ^ {-1/3} \approx 1.1998559643 \lt 2 ^ {1/3}$):**

     ```
     \sum_{(i, j, k) \in I_m^<} (a_i + a_j - a_k) \equiv \sum_{r=1}^m (r^2 - m) a_r,
     ```
     since index $r$ appears $(r-1) + \frac{(m-r)(m+r-3)}{2}$ times as $i$, $(r-1) ^ 2$ times as $j$, and $\frac{(m-r)(m+r-1)}{2}$ times as $k$. Because all $N _ m ^ { \lt } = \frac{m(m-1)(2m-1)}{6}$ integers $w(i, j; k) \ge 2$ on $\mathcal{I} _ m ^ { \lt }$ are pairwise distinct for any $B _ 3$ sequence, $\sum _ {r=1} ^ m (r ^ 2 - m) a _ r \ge \frac{N _ m ^ { \lt }(N _ m ^ { \lt }+3)}{2} = \frac{m ^ 6}{18} + O(m ^ 5){}$; dropping the negative terms $r \lt \sqrt{m}$ under $a _ r \le (r/c) ^ 3(1+o(1)){}$ immediately gives $\frac{m ^ 6}{18} \le \frac{m ^ 6}{6 c ^ 3} \implies \liminf _ {x\to\infty} A(x)/x ^ {1/3} \le 3 ^ {1/3} \approx 1.44224957$.
   - **Part (b) (Concave-Margin Positivity on the Neyman–Pearson Sub-Level Sub-Cone $\Omega _ L ^ \ast$):** Restricting to the sub-level sub-cone $\Omega _ \tau(m) = \lbrace (i, j, k) \in \mathcal{I} _ m ^ { \lt } : i ^ 3 + j ^ 3 - k ^ 3 \le \tau m ^ 3\rbrace$ ($\tau = L ^ {-3} \in (0, 1]$) with rescaled continuum domain $\Omega _ L ^ \ast = \lbrace (X, Y, Z) : 0 \lt X \le Y \le L,\; 0 \lt Z \lt Y,\; X ^ 3 + Y ^ 3 - Z ^ 3 \le 1\rbrace$, proves that the marginal weight $W _ L(U) = W _ X(U) + W _ Y(U) - W _ Z(U) \ge 0$ is pointwise nonnegative for **all** $U \in (0, L]$ and **all** $L \ge 1$ via the strict concavity $\phi''(t) = -\frac{2}{9}t ^ {-5/3} \lt 0$ of $\phi(t) = t ^ {1/3}$ ($2\phi(U ^ 3) - \phi(U ^ 3 - (1-X ^ 3)) - \phi(U ^ 3 + (1-X ^ 3)) \gt 0$ on $U \in [1, (L ^ 3-1) ^ {1/3}]$), eliminating all negative coefficients $r ^ 2 - m \lt 0$ at the source ($\min _ {1 \le r \le m} C _ r(m, \tau) \ge 0$).

In the continuum limit $a _ r \sim c r ^ p$ ($p = 2h - 1 \ge 3$), slot eviction sharpens $\frac{c}{p+3} \ge \frac{1}{18}$ to $\bigl(\frac{1}{p+3} + \frac{1}{2p(p+3)(2p+1)}\bigr)c \ge \frac{1}{2}\bigl(\frac{1}{3} + \frac{1}{6p(p+1)}\bigr) ^ 2$, giving $c \ge c _ p ^ {\mathrm{evict}} := \frac{(p+3)(2p+1)(2p ^ 2+2p+1) ^ 2}{36 p(p+1) ^ 2(4p ^ 2+2p+1)}$ ($c _ 3 ^ {\mathrm{evict}} = \frac{4375}{12384} \approx 0.3532784238 \gt \frac{1}{3}$, lowering $3 ^ {1/3} \approx 1.4422495703$ to $(\frac{12384}{4375}) ^ {1/3} \approx 1.4145803750$).
14. **Theorem 2.13 (Dyadic $(2,1){}$-Shell Vandermonde Decomposition & Hyperfactorial Sawtooth Antiderivative Identity):**
    - **Part (a) (Exact Three-Type Dyadic Shell Vandermonde Decomposition):** For any integers $P \ge 0$ and $m \ge 0$ representing the prefix count $P = A(2 ^ {r-1}){}$ and shell increment $m = m _ r := A(2 ^ r) - A(2 ^ {r-1}){}$ in $I _ r = (2 ^ {r-1}, 2 ^ r]$, the ordered $(2,1){}$-triple increment across shell $r$ splits identically into pure intra-shell $(0,3){}$ triples, $(2,1){}$ prefix-anchor triples, and $(1,2){}$ cross-shell triples:
      ```
      \binom{P + m + 1}{3} - \binom{P + 1}{3} \equiv \binom{m + 1}{3} + m \binom{P + 1}{2} + P \binom{m}{2}.
      ```
      By Theorem 2.3, 100% of the $\binom{m _ r + 1}{3}$ Type $(0,3){}$ intra-shell triples lie strictly inside $\mathcal{T} _ m ^ -$ ($w _ {i,j,k} \le -2$).
    - **Part (b) (Exact Hyperfactorial Sawtooth Antiderivative & Glaisher–Kinkelin Expansion):** Because $A(t) \in \mathbb{Z} _ {\ge 0}$ is integer-valued, the fractional deviation $\lbrace u\rbrace = u - \lfloor u \rfloor$ on each unit step $u \in [n, n+1]$ ($n \ge 1$) contributes $I _ n := \int _ n ^ {n+1} \frac{\lbrace u\rbrace(1-\lbrace u\rbrace)}{u}\,du = (2n+1)\ln(1 + \frac{1}{n}) - 2 = \frac{1}{6n} - \frac{1}{12n ^ 2} + O(n ^ {-3}) \gt 0$. Summing over $1 \le n \le M - 1$ gives the exact closed-form hyperfactorial identity:
      ```
      F_Haar(M) := \int_0^M \frac{\{u\}(1 - \{u\})}{u} du
                 = \frac{M^2}{2} - M(M - 1) \ln M + 2 \sum_{n=1}^{M-1} n \ln n

      ```
      where $H(M-1) = \prod _ {n=1} ^ {M-1} n ^ n$ is the hyperfactorial and $A _ {\mathrm{GK}} \approx 1.2824271291006226$ is the Glaisher–Kinkelin constant ($2\ln A _ {\mathrm{GK}} \approx 0.4975089540675685$).
15. **Theorem 2.14 & Corollary 2.15 (Unconditional Diophantine Haar Sub-Block Variance Closure for Infinite $B _ 3$ and Odd-Order $B _ {2h-1}$ Sequences):**
    Across any window of $K \ge 1$ consecutive dyadic shells $[2 ^ {-K} N, N]$, the $L ^ 2$-orthogonal Haar wavelet decomposition of the integer jump function $A(t) \in \mathbb{Z} _ {\ge 0}$ against the concave-margin sub-level sub-cone weight $W _ L(U) \ge 0$ of Theorem 2.7(b) injects an irreducible Diophantine quantization variance boost of at least $\kappa _ {\mathrm{int}, 3} ^ {\mathrm{Dio}}(K) = \frac{K\ln 2}{3(1 - 4 ^ {-K})}$ (and $\kappa _ {\mathrm{int}, 3} ^ {\mathrm{GK}}(K) = \frac{K\ln 2 + 6 C _ {\mathrm{GK}}}{3(1 - 4 ^ {-K})}$ with the Glaisher–Kinkelin constant), yielding for every $K \ge 1$:
